## Distributing a fork

The AGPL lets you publish a modified app. Before you do, give it your own identity:

1. Set your own name and bundle id prefix in `apps/ios/Config/Local.xcconfig`
   (`APP_DISPLAY_NAME`, `BUNDLE_ID_PREFIX`).
2. Replace the icon (`apps/ios/VSCodeRemote/AppIcon.icon`).
3. Publish your source code, as the AGPL requires.
